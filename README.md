# International Student Uni Data

I wanted to find out which UK university gives international students the best value for a Computer Science degree, comparing tuition fees with world rankings.

## Data

- International_Education_Costs.csv: tuition and living costs for universities around the world (practice dataset)
- Uni_Ranking_1625.csv: world university rankings from 2016 to 2025

## What I did

- Filtered the data down to 3-year Computer Science bachelor's courses in the UK
- Cleaned up university names so the two datasets would match (e.g. UCL vs University College London)
- Merged the tuition data with the 2025 rankings
- Made charts comparing tuition and rank

## Result

Queen Mary University of London came out as the best value: 17th in the UK and the cheapest of the top 20 at $23,500 a year. In general, the higher the ranking, the higher the fees.

## Tools

Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Jupyter
